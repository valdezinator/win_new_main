# Feature Roadmap & Progress Tracker

## 🎵 Already Implemented Features
- Audio playback (background, error handling, just_audio integration)
- Authentication (Google OAuth, Email/Password, Email Sign-up)
- Modern desktop-style UI (sidebar, bottom player, Apple Music-inspired)
- Database: songs, albums, artists, playlists, user sessions
- Dynamic playlists (Daily Mix, mood-based, groundwork for more)
- Payment & subscription (Stripe/PayPal, premium features, UI)
- Audio ads (Spotify-like logic, ad manager, user preferences, analytics)
- Analytics (Firebase Analytics, Sentry, Supabase event logging)
- Offline listening (premium)
- Listening time tracking (for ads, stats)
- Basic social/collaborative listening groundwork
- **Advanced Recommendation Engine** (now includes genre/artist similarity, session duration, and click analytics)

## 🚀 Features To Be Implemented
- **Collaborative Filtering** (Spotify-style, "users who listened to X also liked Y")
- **UI/UX Polish** (magazine-style, gradients, animations)
- **Artist-First Model & Song Credits** (Tidal-style, detailed credits, fair royalties)
- **Audio/Video Integration** (YouTube Music-style, seamless switch, music videos)
- **Indie Artist & Community** (SoundCloud-style uploads, in-track comments, album threads)
- **Voice Assistant Integration** (Amazon Music-style, Windows/Alexa/Google support)
- **SongCatcher & Real-Time Lyrics** (Deezer-style music recognition, synced lyrics, translation)
- **Cross-Platform Playlist Collaboration** (Deezer Shaker-style, invite friends, multi-service)
- **Expanded Social Features** (activity feeds, music taste matching, in-app messaging)
- **Hi-Res Audio & Equalizer** (lossless, custom EQ)
- **User Preferences & Settings** (theme, playback, privacy)
- **Enhanced Analytics & Testing** (crash reporting, revenue, more tests)
